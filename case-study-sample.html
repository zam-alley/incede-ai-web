The customer

Alaska Airlines is one of North America's leading air carriers, operating a complex network of business, operational and customer-facing workflows that depend on accurate, timely insight from across the organization. Like many large enterprises, Alaska's analytics and AI ambitions span multiple teams, platforms, and decision points.

The engagement

Incede.ai serves as a trusted AI and analytics advisor to Alaska — bringing IBM-aligned solutioning, governance discipline, and delivery focus to multi-workstream initiatives. The Incede team partners with Alaska's stakeholders to scope, prioritize, and deliver outcomes that matter to the business.

In the customer's words

"With Incede.ai as our trusted advisor, we have delivered real value to our business stakeholders."

— Alaska Airlines

Why it works

What Alaska gets from Incede is what every long-standing customer of ours gets: a partner that combines IBM-deep technical expertise with a relentless focus on the business outcome. We don't measure ourselves by hours billed; we measure by what shipped, what's running, and what value it created. Across our 200+ customers and 500+ projects, the pattern is consistent — and Alaska is one of many references we're proud of.
