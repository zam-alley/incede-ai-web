The problem

Healthcare's AI agent problem is rarely about the model.

It's about everything around the model — PHI handling, EMR write-back, payer-provider data exchange, the audit trail your compliance officer needs at 4pm on a Friday. The wins in healthcare GenAI don't come from a smarter LLM; they come from agents that respect the constraint of the environment.

Most pilots stall at the first PHI conversation. Many of the rest stall when Epic's interoperability team asks for a signed BAA and a data-flow diagram. We've built that diagram a dozen times. We bring it on day 1.

The result: customers move from "interesting demo" to a production agent that touches PHI, lives behind their IdP, writes back to Epic, and produces evidence packets their auditors recognize — in 6 to 10 weeks.

Healthcare FAQ.

Do you sign a BAA?

Yes. We typically sign within 5 business days; pre-baked template available on request.

Does PHI ever leave our VPC?

No. PHI processing happens within your watsonx tenant, in the region of your choice; redacted inputs are used for fallback model calls when applicable.

Does Orchestrate integrate with Epic?

Yes, natively via FHIR plus Epic's interconnect APIs. We've built that integration in 3 prior engagements.

Can the platform run on-prem?

Yes — watsonx supports on-prem and sovereign-cloud deployment, including OpenShift.
