AI Governance & Trust

Agentic workflows in regulated industries don't ship without a control plane. We deliver the governance layer that makes agents auditable, explainable, and safe to run at scale — built natively on watsonx.governance and aligned to IBM, SOC 2, HIPAA, and GDPR standards.

Policy & access

Role-based access, fine-grained tool permissions, and policy enforcement at the agent boundary. The agent never has more authority than the human invoking it.

Audit & lineage

Full request, response, and tool-call lineage. The evidence packet your auditors and model risk committee will actually recognize.

Drift & accuracy

Golden-set evaluations that re-run on every release. Drift detection across model versions, prompts, and retrieval indexes.

Bias & fairness

Bias monitoring and fairness metrics tuned to your use case — credit, hiring, healthcare access, customer treatment.

Safety guardrails

PII detection, prompt-injection defense, hallucination scoring, harmful-content filters, and refusal patterns for out-of-policy intent.

Cost & latency observability

Per-agent, per-LLM cost tracking and latency SLOs. Wired into Datadog, Splunk, or Instana — whatever your on-call team already uses.
